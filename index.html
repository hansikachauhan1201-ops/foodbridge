Our Purpose & Story

Bridging the Gap Between Surplus and Hunger

Every day in commercial kitchens, perfectly safe, untouched food is discarded simply because of surplus production. Food Bridge connects those who have surplus with those who need sustenance.

The Reality of Food Wastage

In urban centers like Jaipur, caterers, hotels, banquet halls, and multi-cuisine cafes must prepare buffer volumes to guarantee customer satisfaction. When business wraps up, untouched pans of fragrant biryani, curries, freshly baked bread, and produce often have no destination except garbage bins.

Meanwhile, community shelters, children’s homes, and daily-wage colonies located just 3 kilometers away struggle to provide nutritious daily meals. This is not a supply problem—it is a coordination problem.

How Restaurants Contribute

In less than 60 seconds at closing time, chefs log available portions. No paperwork, zero collection hassle, and verifiable social impact records for corporate responsibility.

How NGOs Bridge the Community

Equipped with thermal carriers, verified volunteers receive neighborhood alerts, collect food safely within safe temperature windows, and serve dignified meals with care.

Dignity Over Charity

Surplus food redistribution isn’t about scraps. It’s about sharing delicious, chef-crafted culinary creations so every neighbor eats with pride and nourishment.

100% Compassion Driven

"Throwing away untouched food used to break our kitchen team's heart. Food Bridge turned our surplus into our staff’s proudest evening tradition."

— Chef Ananya Mehta, Jaipur Heritage Bistro

How Food Bridge Works

A seamless, transparent pipeline engineered to move surplus meals from commercial stoves to community tables in under 90 minutes.

01
Step 1: Restaurants

List Surplus Food

Step 2: Food Bridge

Smart Visibility & Match

Food Bridge immediately publishes the batch to our live discovery dashboard and dispatches geotargeted alerts to verified NGOs within 5 km.

Geo-Matched Alerts

Step 3: Registered NGOs

Claim & Pick Up

The assigned NGO claims the listing on the marketplace, coordinates with the kitchen, and dispatches insulated transport containers.

Thermal Chain of Custody

Dignified Distribution

Driving Climate Sustainability

Food waste generates over 8% of all human-induced greenhouse gases globally. Keeping safe food out of landfills is one of the most effective local climate actions.

  • Diverts methane from landfills
  • Conserves water & farm energy
  • CSR compliance for restaurants
