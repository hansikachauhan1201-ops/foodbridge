Environmental Preservation: Saving Resources from Methane Decomposition

When edible food decomposes in landfills, it generates methane—a greenhouse gas 28x more potent than CO2. By redirecting 210,000 kg of food, the Food Bridge model has prevented approximately 525 Metric Tons of CO2 equivalent emissions and preserved over 1.8 Million Liters of virtual water used during agricultural production.

Real Voices

Stories Behind the Numbers

How real-time redistribution transforms excess food from a logistical burden into heartfelt gratitude.

"We used to discard 20 to 30 kg of pristine lunch thali preparations when the afternoon buffet closed. With Food Bridge, local NGO volunteers pick it up within 45 minutes using insulated thermal carriers. It’s an honor to know that food nourishes children every single day."

Chef Rajesh Sharma
Executive Chef, Royal Jaipur Thali Lounge

"As an NGO operating three night shelters in Jaipur, our daily challenge was balancing grocery costs. Food Bridge gives us dignified access to fresh wholesome meals from bakeries and restaurants. The transparent claim system makes coordination seamless."

Pooja Meena
Shelter Director, Jaipur Aashray Foundation

"Bakery goods have a strict shelf life. Before Food Bridge, surplus loaves went to waste. Now, evening surpluses are claimed in minutes by community kitchens. We receive automated impact certificates for our CSR documentation."

Vikramaditya Rathore
Founder, The French Crust Bakery
