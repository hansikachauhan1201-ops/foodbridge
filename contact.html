Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about food safety, pickup coordination, and zero-fee redistribution.

Commercial establishments can donate surplus, safe, and untouched cooked food (such as buffet trays of dal, paneer curries, steamed rice, and phulkas), artisan bakery products (multigrain loaves, burger buns, rolls), grade-A fresh produce, and unexpired packaged food. We strictly do not accept half-eaten leftovers, individual plate remainders, or items that failed thermal food storage safety standards.

Food Bridge uses proximity-based notification. When a donor in Malviya Nagar or Mansarovar posts a surplus batch, partner NGOs and verified volunteer drivers in that exact neighborhood receive instant alerts. Across Jaipur, the average pickup arrival time is between 35 and 60 minutes, ensuring food is collected well before the declared pickup deadline.

No. Food Bridge is 100% free for both food donors and non-profit charities. There are zero platform commissions, zero registration fees, and zero distribution charges. The project is created as a civic social-impact and sustainability initiative.

Safety is our highest priority. Donors are required to record the exact cooking/preparation timestamp and certify that hot foods have been continuously maintained in steam vessels above 65°C and cold items below 5°C. Partner NGOs arrive equipped with insulated food-grade containers and thermal bags to preserve food freshness during transit.

We welcome university students and local citizens! Volunteers can assist with afternoon and evening food collection rounds, conduct hygiene inspections, or coordinate distribution at night shelters and community centers. Send an inquiry via the form above selecting "Volunteer Logistics & Driver" to get connected.
